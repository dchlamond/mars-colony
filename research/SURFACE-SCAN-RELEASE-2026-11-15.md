# Walk on Mars: scanning and visual polish

- Press F or click **Scan** near a teaching rock, ground sample or supported equipment. A 2.4-second blue scan sweep, progress display, world-space ring and instrument glow play before the discovery result.
- Escape, walking away, opening another modal, hiding the tab, changing mission or leaving Walk cancels the scan. Rewards are only awarded on completion, once per discovery type. Blackout discoveries retain their no-reward rule.
- A thin rusty-orange veil and 18 lightweight drifting motes appear only in Walk, beneath the HUD. Reduced motion disables moving dust and scan sweep.
- Astronaut materials now follow the selected intro's warm off-white garment, graphite joints/dark visor and small blue accents. Existing Blender geometry and walking/working animations remain; this is a simplified visual match, not a reconstruction of the generated film character.
