DEAD RECKONING: BLACKOUT

Your mission guide

NASA dataset edition · 15 November 2026

Start playing

No account is required. Choose Blackout for a short two-futures experiment, Junior to grow a colony, or Commander for deeper resource management.

The bottom controls pause or run time at speeds 1–5. Space pauses and resumes. Build is available in every mode. Open Crew for staffing and Appeal, Systems for your reserves, and Explore for terrain and the discovery ledger.

One mission, two futures

Predict which preparation will help during the scripted power challenge. Choose a battery, panel alignment, or saving your Parts. Protect your six crew until day nine, compare two MOLA terrain locations, then replay with a different preparation. The starting conditions and event sequence remain the same, so you can explain what your decision changed.

Walk and investigate

Choose Walk on Mars. Use WASD to move; drag or click to look, and Escape to release the mouse. Aim at an individual rock, then press F or E and hold your aim during the scan. Back to map is at the top.

The scan retrieves a NASA MOLA terrain sample at that map location. It shows sample coordinates and approximate elevation from the bundled 1-degree, 8-bit global grid. It does not identify the composition of that individual rock. Scanning adds a ledger record and gives no supplies or Fame.

Keep your colony running

Power machines, maintain air, water and food, and provide beds and shelter. Build a Water Processor to recycle water and add the supply described on its card. Production amounts, capacities and event consequences are game rules, not NASA measurements.

Appeal rewards internal living conditions. Fame affects colony awareness. A crew arrival has a countdown and arrivals are distributed among available colonies. Private multiplayer rooms give each classmate a separate colony; the host controls the shared clock.

Check the evidence

  • NASA MOLA: planetary elevation, coordinates and terrain comparison.
  • NASA/JPL Horizons: dated Earth–Mars distances and one-way light times.
  • NASA DONKI: dated event records, observing instruments and source identifiers. Earth observations do not establish a Mars radiation dose or forecast.

Resources, characters, competition, buildings, artwork and mission schedules are designed game elements. There are no invented ice deposits, cave bonuses or rock-content rewards. The former radiation-index tool and day-300 escalating storms are removed. NASA does not endorse this game.
