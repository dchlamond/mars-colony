Classroom playtest

Open the game in a browser. No account is needed. Choose Blackout for a controlled comparison, Junior for colony growth or Commander for more management.

  1. Ask students to predict which preparation will protect the crew.
  2. Run the challenge, then replay with one changed choice.
  3. Compare actual MOLA records from two locations. Keep their coordinates and elevations together.
  4. Open Data sources: distinguish MOLA elevations, Horizons calculations and Earth-observed DONKI events.
  5. Explain which outcomes depend on designed game rules. Resource units and storm consequences are not measured astronaut-survival predictions.

Testing checklist

Check Build, Systems, Crew and Explore; bottom speed controls and Space pause; saving and continuing; Walk on Mars and F/E scans; separate colonies in a private classroom room. Record the mode, browser, action and result if anything fails.
