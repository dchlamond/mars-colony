DEAD RECKONING: BLACKOUT

One mission.
Two futures.

A short playable tour of a Mars outpost for ages 7–12. The player predicts, prepares, observes and compares. NASA data helps explain the choices, while the simulation makes their consequences visible.

Play the judge tourOpen the full game

The tour uses the normal six-person mission and costs. Its prepared outpost is labelled as a demonstration; it grants no extra rewards or supplies. Allow roughly three minutes for this directed walkthrough. A child's guided session may take longer.

  1. Predict and prepare.Choose Begin mission. Predict whether stored energy or more generation will help, then build a battery. Compare the two simulated shelter readings on the map, choose the soil shelter, and request Earth advice. Open Systems to inspect the actual air, water and power flows.
  2. Lead while Earth is quiet.Advance one day at a time. The sourced light-time appears beside a clearly compressed training clock. Radio silence pauses advice; the separately scheduled dust changes solar generation. Your crew and machines reflect their real assignments and operating state.
  3. Change one decision.At the outcome, compare solar alignment instead. Replay the same later requests. Compare both results and choose your explanation for the difference. Review the feedback, answer the next-mission question, then print or download the anonymous mission record. The first attempt stays separate from practice.
What to look for

A consequence that comes from the same game rules, a repeatable comparison, and a child explaining the evidence. These are implemented gameplay features. Automated testing checks behaviour; it does not establish learning gains or replace a child pilot.
