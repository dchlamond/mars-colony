## Short description
Run a Mars colony where every new arrival needs air, food and shelter. Choose where to build on a map based on NASA elevation data, prepare for recorded solar storms and compare the consequences of different plans.

## What works
The prototype includes colony resources, construction, four landing sites, recorded storm scenarios, rivals, lessons, source screens and an explorable surface. The local strategy-interface revision adds resource trends, panels, map lenses, construction queue and device-local saving. Check the current public build before recording the demonstration.

## Data and limits
MOLA-derived elevation informs the map. Archived DONKI and Curiosity references inform existing storm scenarios. Sunlight and air thickness are simplified models; ice and caves are regional rules, not surveyed deposits. Equipment rates, costs, colonist behavior and rewards are fictional game rules. The optional weather panel is separate from the historical storm simulation. NASA does not endorse the game.
